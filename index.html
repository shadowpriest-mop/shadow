About Mind Flay: Insanity

Mind Flay: Insanity is a proc that makes your next Mind Flay instant and increases its damage. To maximize this proc, you want to avoid refreshing DoTs during the buff window when possible.

Tip: Refresh your DoTs early (with ~30% duration remaining) if a Mind Flay: Insanity proc is about to occur. This prevents having to refresh during the buff, maximizing your damage window.
